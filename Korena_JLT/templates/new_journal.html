HOW IT WORKS

Record once, update everywhere.

  1. Choose the accounts affected by your transaction.
  2. Enter an amount in either Debit or Credit for each line.
  3. When the totals match, post the journal. The ledger and trial balance calculate from these lines.
Example: Cash sale of ₱2,000 → Debit Cash ₱2,000 and Credit Sales Revenue ₱2,000.
